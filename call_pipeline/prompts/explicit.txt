Classify the call from explicit evidence. Distinguish the customer's goal from
what actually happened. A request for help does not mean a consultation was booked.
Use consultation_scheduled only when both parties agreed on a consultation time;
an unanswered offer or an ordinary callback is follow_up_needed.
A stop-contact request takes priority over an earlier sales inquiry for both
primary_intent and call_outcome. Keep the earlier inquiry as secondary_intent.
Do not infer eligibility from a dollar amount. Use needs_review for potential
service requests, existing_customer for an explicit existing case, otherwise
not_applicable or unknown as the taxonomy describes.
Unknown urgency is preferable to guessing. Use neutral sentiment if wording
does not support another label. Text does not preserve audio tone.
Choose one compliance_flag: prioritize a do_not_contact request, then a guarantee,
individualized tax advice, coercion, and finally transcription problems.
PII detection is handled separately; redaction placeholders are not compliance flags.
Use a lower confidence for ambiguous or incomplete calls. This is a subjective
estimate, not a probability calibrated against real outcomes.
Notes must explain the decision in at most two short sentences without identifiers.
